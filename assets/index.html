01 · About us

Not a class. A community.

Looking for a place to bring your ideas to life? Our writing club is a supportive community where members explore creative prompts, share feedback, and collaborate on school publications. Any student with a passion for storytelling can join us.

Creative prompts

Fresh ideas every session to get the words flowing, from poetry to short fiction.

Supportive feedback

Share your writing and get kind, useful thoughts from fellow members.

School publications

Work together on pieces that reach the whole school community.
